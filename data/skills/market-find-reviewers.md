## When to use

The user has something to launch and needs coverage from people whose audience
already cares about the category.

## Find by what they have covered

Search for reviews and coverage of **comparable products by name**. Anyone who
covered a comparable has demonstrated both interest and an audience for it.
That is a far stronger signal than follower count.

Cover the formats separately: written reviews and blogs, YouTube, podcasts,
newsletters, and social accounts that review rather than repost. Each reaches
a different slice.

## Qualify

For each:

- **Do they cover independent work?** Many outlets only cover major releases.
  This is the single biggest filter and it is usually obvious from the last
  ten items they covered.
- **Are they active?** Check the most recent post date. Dormant outlets are
  most of any list built by search.
- **Audience size and, more importantly, engagement.** Comments and replies
  tell you more than subscriber counts.
- **Do they accept submissions?** Many publish review policies — format
  accepted, lead time needed, whether physical copies are required.

## Record what the pitch needs

- Name, outlet, format, contact method (the one they ask for, not the one that
  is easiest)
- Their stated review policy and lead time
- **Two or three specific things they covered** that are genuinely comparable
- The angle: why this particular product suits this particular outlet

That last field is the deliverable. A pitch that names something they actually
covered and explains the connection gets read; a mass email does not.

## Respect lead times

Reviewers with an audience plan weeks or months out. A pitch sent at launch is
too late for most of them. Note each outlet's lead time and work backwards
from the launch date — that scheduling is often the difference between coverage
and none.

## Report

A sheet sorted by fit: outlet, person, format, audience, activity, policy,
lead time, contact, comparable coverage, angle. Plus a suggested send order —
long-lead outlets first.

## Heuristics

- One outlet that covers your exact niche beats ten large general ones.
- Never send the same pitch twice. The angle field exists so you do not have to.
- If they publish a policy, follow it exactly. Ignoring it is an instant no.

## Gotchas

Contact addresses on old sites are frequently dead. Prefer the contact method
they currently point to — often a form or a social DM rather than the email in
the footer.
