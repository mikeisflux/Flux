## When to use

Before outreach or a first meeting, establish whether an account is worth the effort and what to lead with.

## Qualify before you research

Most research effort is spent on accounts that were never a fit. Check fit first - size, sector, geography, and whether they use something you integrate with or replace. If it fails fit, stop and write one line saying why.

## Then build the picture

- **What they sell and to whom.** In their words, from their site.
- **Shape** - headcount, funding, growth direction. Hiring in a function is the clearest signal of investment in it.
- **The trigger** - a raise, a launch, a new executive, a regulation, a public problem. Outreach without a trigger is a cold email with extra steps.
- **The map** - who owns the problem, who signs, who blocks. Name them.
- **Current state** - what they use today for the job you would do.

## What good output looks like

Half a page per account: fit verdict with a reason, the trigger with a date and a link, two named people with roles, and a one-sentence hypothesis of the problem you would solve. Anything longer is not read.

## Gotchas

- Company size from a directory is usually stale and usually high. Cross-check against the careers page or LinkedIn headcount.
- A parent company's news is not the subsidiary's news.
- Record where each fact came from. Half of prospect research is wrong within a quarter and the source is how you tell.
