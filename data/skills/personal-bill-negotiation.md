## When to use

A recurring bill - broadband, mobile, insurance, energy - is higher than it needs to be, and the job is to get it down without losing the service.

## Know the number before calling

Three things, all findable in ten minutes:

- **What you pay now**, including anything that rolled off a discount.
- **What they charge a new customer** for the same thing today. This is the number that does the work.
- **What a competitor charges**, with the specific plan name.

Also check whether you are in contract and what the exit fee is. Negotiating without that is negotiating blind.

## Ask plainly, once

"My contract has ended and I'm paying £X. You're offering the same package to new customers at £Y, and [competitor] is at £Z. Can you match it?" Then stop talking.

Most first-line agents have a fixed discount they can apply without escalation. If the first answer is no, ask to be put through to retentions or cancellations - that is where the real authority sits, and asking for it directly is normal and expected.

## Be willing to actually leave

The leverage is entirely in being prepared to switch. Know the switching cost and the effort before the call, so that if the answer is no you can say yes to leaving. A threat you would not carry out is read accurately and produces nothing.

## Close it properly

- Get the new price, what it includes, how long it lasts, and any new contract term.
- Get it in writing - email or the account page - before hanging up.
- Diarise the date the discount ends. That date is when this happens again, and forgetting it is how the bill crept up in the first place.

## Gotchas

- A lower price with a longer contract or a worse allowance is not a saving; compare the total over the term.
- Loyalty is not rewarded by default in any of these markets. The discount goes to people who ask.
- Be polite. The person on the phone did not set the price and has discretion they will not use for someone who is rude.
- Never share full card details or a password to "verify"; use the account's own verification.
