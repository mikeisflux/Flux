## When to use

Getting a document signed, in the right order, by the right people, without a re-send.

## Check the document before touching the signature tool

Right version, right parties named exactly as their legal entities, all blanks filled, all schedules attached, dates and amounts correct. A document sent for signature with a placeholder in it is re-sent, and every re-send costs days and credibility.

## Get the signatories right

Confirm each person has authority to bind their organisation for this value and this type of agreement. The most common delay is discovering at the end that the person who signed could not. Check the counterparty's signatory too, politely, before sending.

## Set up the routing deliberately

- **Order** - sequential where one party's signature is conditional on another's, parallel where it is not. Parallel is faster and is the right default.
- **Fields** - signature, date and initials placed on every page that needs them, assigned to the correct signer.
- **CC** the people who need the executed copy but do not sign.
- **Reminders and expiry** set explicitly rather than left at the default.

## Gotchas

- Verify email addresses out of band for anything of value; a signature request sent to a compromised or wrong address is a serious problem.
- File the executed copy in the contract repository immediately, with the key dates extracted. An executed contract that only exists in someone's inbox is a contract nobody will find.
- Some documents cannot be signed electronically in some jurisdictions. Check before, not after.
